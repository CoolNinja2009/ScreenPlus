# Rainy Window
<img src="preview.gif" width="300" />

Animated Rainy Window wallpaper for [Lively Wallpaper](https://github.com/rocksdanister/lively)

[Download](https://github.com/rocksdanister/rain/releases)

[Live Demo](https://www.rocksdanister.com/rain)

#### Features
- Custom image or video background.
- Rain particle intensity, speed, size control and lightning toggle.
- Background color temperature (post processing.)
- Background panning animation.
- Background mouse parallax.
- Background blur.
- Framerate control.

#### Changes in this copy (Screen+)
Modified from the original under the same licence (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0):
- Clearer glass: less fog by default, and every drop and trail is a clear window through it.
- The background in 3D: it moves in depth with the mouse, using a depth map per picture (`depth/<picture>.png`,
  made with Screen+'s depth model).
- Weather inside the scene, placed by depth: fast rain falls in front of the far mountains, not in front of the
  near ground, with drifting veils of rain in the distance.
- A Weather option (Rain or Snow): snow drifts and sways in depth (six-armed crystals of every shape that spin and tumble, and round clumps), with frost on the glass.
- Drops landing: the picture becomes a rough 3D surface; each drop falls until it meets it (a hood, a shoulder, a ridge, the ground) and a few of those landings glint, anime style (flakes do the same and some settle where they land as soft dots that melt).
- Rain that is never even: heavier and lighter spells, gusts sweeping across the scene, wind that sways its slant; every
  layer moves with the 3D view at its own distance.
