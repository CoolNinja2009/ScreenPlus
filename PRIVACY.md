# Screenplus Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 27 November 2026*

Screenplus (Screen+) is a desktop wallpaper app developed by CoolNinja2009. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What we collect

Nothing. Screenplus has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or advertising. It sends no personal information to the developer or anyone else.

## What stays on your PC

Your wallpaper library, settings, wallpaper settings, previews and logs (including crash reports) are stored only in the app's local data folder and are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## When Screenplus goes online

Only when you use a feature that needs it:

- **Store page:** browsing and downloading wallpapers loads pages and files from the wallpaper sites it lists (for example Wallhaven, MoeWalls and MotionBGs). Those sites receive normal web requests, including your IP address, under their own privacy policies.
- **Importing from a link:** fetches the video or page you entered from its website. The first time, it also downloads the helper tools it needs (yt-dlp and QuickJS) from GitHub.
- **AI depth and upscaling:** the first time you use them, the AI models are downloaded from Hugging Face and the ONNX Runtime from NuGet. Your pictures are processed on your PC and never uploaded.
- **Updates (copies not installed from the Microsoft Store):** checks GitHub or GitLab for new releases and sample wallpapers.
- **Web wallpapers:** a wallpaper that is a web page can load content from the internet, like any web page. Only use web wallpapers from sources you trust.

## Audio and microphone

For audio-reactive wallpapers, Screenplus reads the sound your PC is playing, on your PC only. "Mute while these apps use the microphone" only checks whether an app is using the microphone. Screenplus never records, stores or sends any audio.

## Children

Screenplus collects no data from anyone, including children.
